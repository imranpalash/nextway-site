Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Nextway Education Consultancy, with offices in Tangail, Dhaka and Sakhipur, Bangladesh. Our main contact address is Choto Kalibari Road, Akur Takur Para, Tangail 1900. You can reach us at info@nextwayedu.com or on +880 1614 109215.

What we collect

Contact form

If you use our contact form, we receive the details you type in: your name, phone number, email address (optional), the service you are interested in, your preferred country (optional) and your message. We use this only to reply to you and to advise you on the services you asked about.

Server logs

Our web host automatically records technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request. This is used to keep the site secure and working properly.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Third-party services

Google Maps

The contact page embeds a Google Maps view. When that map loads, Google may receive your IP address and set cookies, under Google’s own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram

Links to WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram take you to those services. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave our site.

How we use and keep your data

We use your details to respond to enquiries and to provide the services you request. We do not sell your data. We share it only where needed to deliver a service you have asked for (for example, with a university or embassy as part of an application you have authorised), or where the law requires it. We keep enquiry data only as long as needed for those purposes.

Your rights

You may ask us at any time what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email info@nextwayedu.com and we will respond promptly. If you consented to us contacting you, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
