Questions

Things students ask us first.

Can’t find yours? Call us on +880 1614 109215 or drop in.

What services does Nextway offer?

Study abroad consultation, IELTS and Spoken English courses, university applications, visa support, student air tickets and help after you arrive.

Is consultation really free?

No. Consultation is free. Come in, call or message us, tell us your goals and we will give you an honest view of what is realistic, with no obligation. Fees apply only to the services you choose, and we explain them up front.

How do you prepare students for IELTS?

Our classes of up to 10 students cover Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, with full-length mock tests so you can track your progress.

Which universities can you help me apply to?

We work with a wide network of universities across Europe, the UK, North America, Oceania and Asia. Share your course and budget and we will shortlist the best matches.

How does visa assistance work?

We audit your documents and finances, file your application, and coach you through a mock interview. If you were refused before, we study the refusal letter and rebuild your case.

What help do I get after I arrive?

We help with airport pickup, housing, local orientation and opening a bank account, so you settle in quickly.

Where are your offices and when are they open?

We have offices in Tangail (Choto Kalibari Road, Akur Takur Para), Dhaka (Akram Tower, Bijoy Nagar Road) and Sakhipur (Muktijuddha Complex, Kacha Bazar). Every office is open 10 am to 7 pm, every day except Friday.
